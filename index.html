Privacy Policy for Sudoku Hero

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Sudoku Hero processes information to provide the game, maintain accounts, measure app use, and offer optional rewarded ads.

  • Account information, such as your email address, username, and in-game user ID. If you use Apple or Google sign-in, we receive the account identifiers and profile information provided by that service.
  • Game information, such as progress, scores, achievements, inventory, friends, and challenges.
  • Purchase records, including product and transaction identifiers, receipts, amounts, and verification results, to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate or fraudulent claims.
  • App activity and technical information, such as screen views, puzzle activity, purchases, app version, device model, operating system, and diagnostic events.
  • Notification tokens, if you enable push notifications, to deliver game notifications.
  • Ad-related information, such as ad interactions, reward attempts, verification results, and consent choices. Google and other SDK providers may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, and diagnostic information, depending on the service, device permissions, and privacy choices. IP addresses may be used to estimate an approximate location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to authenticate accounts, save progress, provide social features and notifications, process purchases, verify ad rewards, prevent fraud, understand app performance, improve the game, and measure how people find and use Sudoku Hero.

We use Amplitude for app analytics and AppsFlyer for installation and marketing attribution. Events may include registration, screen or puzzle activity, tutorial completion, and purchases. These services can associate events with your in-game user ID. The app disables Amplitude's IP-address tracking option; this does not prevent our servers or other providers from processing network information.

3. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store and Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information, such as credit card numbers. Purchase validation is performed with the respective app store to confirm transactions and deliver in-game currency.

4. Optional Rewarded Ads

Where available, you can choose to watch an ad in Shop to earn crystals. We use Google AdMob to provide these ads. The app requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

To verify a reward, the app sends Google a signed, short-lived reward token containing your in-game user ID and a unique reward-attempt identifier. This token is not your account login token. Google sends a signed verification callback to our server so we can credit the correct account and prevent duplicate rewards.

The app uses Google's consent system before requesting ads and checks whether ads may be requested under the current consent status. Where Google's consent system requires privacy options, select Ad privacy choices in Shop to revisit those choices. Availability of a form and the choices it offers depend on your region and Google's configuration.

On iOS, you can manage app tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Denying tracking permission limits access to Apple's advertising identifier. It does not stop all analytics, attribution, or ad-related data processing. Google ad privacy choices and Apple's tracking permission are separate controls.

5. Data Sharing

We share information with providers that support the features described above, including hosting and database providers, Apple and Google for sign-in and purchases, notification delivery providers, Amplitude, AppsFlyer, and Google AdMob. Relevant game information, such as your username and game results, may also be visible to other players through social and leaderboard features.

For information about how these providers handle data, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's information about partner apps, Amplitude's Privacy Notice, and AppsFlyer's Services Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and game information to provide app functionality. Purchase validation and fraud-prevention records may remain after account deletion. Records held by app stores and other service providers are also subject to their retention policies. Contact us with questions about a particular record or a deletion request.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us. You can also delete your account in the app through Profile > Options > Account > Delete Account. Deleting your account does not automatically delete every record held by app stores or other service providers.

You can manage notification permission in your device settings. Rewarded ads are optional. Where available, use the ad privacy choices described above to review Google's privacy form. The app does not currently offer a separate switch for all Amplitude or AppsFlyer analytics and attribution.

8. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Hero is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
